I perceive my role as a bridge between people and nature, using spatial design to bring them closer together. I specialise in urban landscapes, driven by a specific goal: to actively contribute to the rewilding of our cities.

Selected public space, playground and urban landscape projects for Gemeente Utrecht and Buro Sant en Co, and academic work from Delft and Shenzhen.
