Six ways to animate the mark

All six use the vector out of logo2.ai — the curves and the gradients are read from the file, not redrawn. The ink is your CMYK black as it converts for screen, rgb(55,55,55). Tell me a number.

01

1 → 2 → 3, cross-fading

The three forms are one object turning into the light, so it just plays them in order. Nothing is ever distorted — at every instant what is on screen is exactly one of your drawings.

02

still / hover / press

Form 1 at rest, form 2 when the pointer is on it, form 3 while pressed. No loop: it only moves when you do. The most like a real lens.

03

all three at once

The three forms overlap at reduced weight, each turning its light at its own rate. Reads as depth rather than as a sequence.

04

the aperture breathes

Form 2 alone. The outline never moves; the lit core opens and closes inside it, the way an aperture does.

05

the light orbits

Form 2 alone, the gradient centre pushed off the middle and orbiting. The 2D silhouette is frozen and the inside turns in 3D — the thing you asked for, at its plainest.

06

1 → 2 → 3, and the light orbits

The cross-fade with the orbit under it. The fullest version, and the one that departs furthest from the flat drawing.
