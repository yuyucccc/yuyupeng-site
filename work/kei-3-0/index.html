KEI 3.0

Kanaleneiland, Utrecht2025–ongoing

Three street reconstructions running in parallel, designed as one continuous landscape.

KEI 3.0 — KanalenEiland Integraal — is the team behind the reconstruction of the public space of Kanaleneiland, a post-war expansion district in the south-west of Utrecht. Three areas run in parallel: Afrikalaan e.o., Van Heuven Goedhartlaan e.o. and Kanaleneiland Zuid Midden, each at a different point in the planning process.

Running the three together makes it possible to treat the district as one landscape rather than three separate projects. The ambition is that standing in the street you should not be able to tell where one plan area ends and the next begins.

Within an integrated design team — green, traffic, landscape, cables and pipes, soil, water and sustainability — we work on the streets and the courtyards between the blocks: continuous cycle routes, room for trees above ground and below it, and rainwater kept in the district.
