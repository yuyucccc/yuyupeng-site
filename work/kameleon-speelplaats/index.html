Kameleon speelplaats

Pijlsweerd, Utrecht2024–2025

A worn-out playground in one of Utrecht's densest quarters, rebuilt as ground rather than pavement.

De Kameleon sits on the Balkstraat in Pijlsweerd, a dense nineteenth-century quarter where private gardens are rare and the street is the only open space most children have. The existing playground had worn out — hard surfacing, ageing equipment, hardly any shade.

The new design trades paving for ground. Rainwater is held in the soil instead of drained away, the existing trees are kept and given room, and play is redistributed by age: a water play area in sand for the youngest, grass mounds and a climbing tower beyond.

Almost everything that could be reused was reused — the paving bricks, the seating edges stacked from existing concrete tiles, and half of the play equipment, relocated rather than replaced.
